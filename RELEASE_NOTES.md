ImagePilot 1.5.75 更新要点

本次为体验优化版，修复多处「看不到最新内容」的问题：

• 时刻秀封面：现在创建和编辑时选择的封面都能正常保存生效，不再回退到第一张。
• 按城市：首页「按城市」优先展示你最近去过的城市，最新城市不再被淹没；城市较多时点「显示更多」查看完整列表。点「重新检测」会增量扫描新照片并自动补全新城市（只处理没扫过的照片，速度快）。
• 最新发现照片：不再出现长期空白——即使没有新扫描到的照片，也会自动展示你最近的照片。
• 最近照片更全：截图、下载、微信/QQ 等保存的图片现在也会一并出现在「最新发现照片」中，不再只显示相机拍摄的照片（Android）。
• 位置补全后，新城市即时显示，无需重启应用。

感谢使用 ImagePilot，欢迎在「设置 → 检查更新」获取最新版本。

ImagePilot 1.5.75 — What's New

Experience fixes focused on always surfacing your latest content:

• Showcase cover: the cover you pick when creating or editing now saves correctly instead of reverting to the first frame.
• By City: the home "By City" section now surfaces recently visited cities first; tap "Show more" for the full list. "Re-check" now runs an incremental scan that ingests new photos and resolves their cities (only unscanned photos are processed, so it stays fast).
• Recently Discovered: no longer stays blank — it falls back to your most recent photos when there's nothing newly scanned.
• More complete recents: screenshots, downloads and images saved from social apps now also appear in Recently Discovered (Android), not just camera shots.
• New cities show up immediately after location enrichment, no app restart needed.
