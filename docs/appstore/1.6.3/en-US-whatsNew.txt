This update makes classification rules easier to customize and improves photo-library organization.

• Improve joint matching of custom rules and scenarios, and recognize product detail and promotional graphics.
• Edit built-in category rules, add visual scenarios and exclusions, or restore defaults. Custom categories are preserved.
• Refine the distinction between a main subject and its background. Swimming descriptions have been removed from the default Sports & Fitness rules.
• Choose a model when reclassifying photos. Download supported local models or select an online service you have configured.
• Simplify model selection for unclassified photos and videos.
• Select consecutive photos with a long press and drag. Fix selection state remaining after leaving a category.
• Retry videos classified as Other using additional frames.
• Export classification diagnostics as a text file.
• Improve protection during database upgrades and scan failures. Backups now include edited built-in rules.

Updating does not automatically reclassify existing photos. New rules apply to future or explicitly requested classification. Your edited rules are preserved.
